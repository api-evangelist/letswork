# Letswork

> Letswork is a flexible workspace booking platform offering coworking spaces,
> meeting rooms, private offices, and creative studios. Members access 1030+
> workspaces in 63+ cities across 18+ countries via a credit-based monthly
> membership, with no long-term contracts.

## At a glance
- 1030+ workspaces across 63+ cities and 18+ countries
- 80,000+ members
- 4.8 / 5 from 58,163+ ratings across the App Store, Play Store, Google and in-app (aggregate store rating); 24 featured member testimonials are shown on-site at /wall-of-love
- Founded 2019, headquartered in Dubai, UAE
- Monthly memberships from AED 225; free trial available

## Countries served
United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Pakistan, Portugal, Spain, Bahrain,
Lebanon, Jordan, Turkey, Germany, South Africa, Senegal, Ghana, Nigeria, Zambia,
Switzerland, Cote D'Ivoire.

## Products
- Coworking memberships (individuals): credit-based plans, unused credits roll
  over month-to-month while the membership is active.
- Team plans: shared credit pool with admin dashboard, member management, and
  usage analytics.
- Day passes: one-off access without a subscription.
- Meeting rooms: hourly booking across partner venues.
- Private offices: long-term offices via the offices marketplace.
- Creative studios: podcast, photography, and events spaces.

## Pricing
Plans are quoted in local currency at checkout. Indicative AED tiers:
- Free Trial — 15 credits (worth AED 75), 7 days, no card required
- Monthly individual plans starting from AED 225
- Yearly plans include 2 months free
- Team plans available with custom credit pools
- Enterprise: contact sales
